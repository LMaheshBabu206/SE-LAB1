# Lab 2 – Backlog and Sprint Plan

## Epics

1. **Domain Inventory & Administration** – maintain monitored domains and SSL endpoints.
2. **Automated Domain & SSL Auditing** – perform daily WHOIS/RDAP checks and TLS certificate audits.
3. **Expiry Alerts & Escalation** – detect upcoming expiry and route threshold-based alerts.
4. **Alert Review & Accountability** – review alert history and acknowledge alerts with an audit trail.

## Sprint allocation

**Sprint 1 – 15 story points**
- Add a monitored domain – 3
- Update or disable a monitored domain – 2
- Run daily WHOIS/RDAP audit – 5
- Audit SSL/TLS certificate expiry – 5

**Sprint 2 – 15 story points**
- Evaluate certificate expiry thresholds – 3
- Escalate domain expiry alerts – 5
- Send notification through gateway – 3
- View alert history – 2
- Acknowledge an alert – 2
